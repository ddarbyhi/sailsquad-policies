Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Effective October 30, 2026

1. About Sail Squad
Sail Squad helps skippers and crew in Hawaii manage race rosters, invitations, and RSVPs. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect
Account details: full name, email address, and password (passwords are stored in encrypted form).
Contact details: mobile phone number and preferred contact method.
Sailing profile: home island, years of experience, positions, safety certifications, and US Sailing ID.
Emergency contact name and phone number, if you choose to add them.
Race activity: invitations, RSVP responses, and boat and race details.
Consent records: the date you accepted this policy and the date you opted in to text messages.
3. How we use your information
To create and secure your account.
To show your sailing profile to skippers and to let you receive race invitations.
To send race invitations, reminders, and updates in the app and, if you opted in, by text message.
To record RSVPs and keep race rosters accurate.
To contact your listed emergency contact in a safety situation.
4. Who we share it with
Skippers can see the profiles of crew they invite or who respond to their races. Emergency contact details are visible to skippers only.
Service providers that run the app: Supabase (hosting and database) and Twilio (delivery of text messages).
We do not sell your information, and we do not share your phone number for marketing.
5. Text message (SMS) terms
Program: Sail Squad sends text messages about race invitations, RSVP requests, and race updates to the mobile number on your profile.
Consent is optional. Agreeing is not a condition of creating an account or using the app. You can use the app in full without texts.
Message frequency varies with your race activity.
Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
Reply STOP to any message to cancel texts at any time. You will receive one confirmation message. Reply HELP for help, or contact us.
You can also turn texts off by unchecking the box in your profile.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
6. Keeping and deleting your information
You can update your profile at any time. To close your account and have your personal information deleted, contact the Sail Squad administrator through the app. We keep race history only as long as needed to run the season and resolve disputes.

7. Security
Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access to records is restricted to the people and services described above.

8. Changes to this policy
If we change this policy in a way that affects how your information is used, we will ask you to review and accept the update.
